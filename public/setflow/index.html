FAQ

Quick answers before you download.

Is SetFlow available on iPhone, iPad, and Mac?

Yes — SetFlow runs on iPhone and iPad (iOS / iPadOS 17 or later) and as a Mac desktop app (macOS 14 or later, Mac Catalyst). Enable iCloud sync in Settings (Pro) to keep your library across devices, or use Export/Import backup for a file transfer.

Which lyric formats does SetFlow support?

ChordPro (inline chords in brackets) and classic chords-over-lyrics (chord line above the lyric line). You can set capo frets 1–12 per song and transpose in Performance without rewriting the chart.

Does SetFlow include a tuner, metronome, and auto-scroll?

Yes. The guitar tuner is available from home and Settings (and from Performance). Pitch detection stays on device. In Performance you also get a metronome locked to each song’s BPM and optional lyric auto-scroll when tempo and duration are set — plus options to keep the screen awake and lock landscape.

How does iCloud sync work?

With SetFlow Pro, turn on Sync with iCloud while signed into iCloud. Songs, flows, folders, custom chords, and selected settings merge across your devices (including deletes). Use Sync Now anytime; Settings shows usage against Apple’s ~1 MB sync store limit. Large libraries can still use file backups.

How do I back up or move my library?

Pro users: Settings → Export all data to save a backup, then Import to replace or merge on another device. Merge/replace restores songs even if they were previously deleted on that device. With iCloud sync on, your library also stays updated automatically.

Is SetFlow free?

Yes to start — free includes up to 3 songs plus writing, Performance tools, tuner, and metronome. SetFlow Pro unlocks unlimited songs, iCloud sync, and full backups via monthly, annual, or lifetime plans in the App Store.

What is the difference between lifetime and subscription?

Indicative EU pricing: Annual €29.99/year, Monthly €4.99/month, Lifetime €44.99 one-time. Subscriptions may include a free trial when Apple offers one in your storefront. Final prices appear in the App Store.

Can I redeem an offer code?

Yes. Use Redeem Offer Code on the Pro paywall or in Settings. Codes are Apple offer codes — they unlock a real App Store purchase (subscription or product) managed by Apple. You can also restore purchases with the same Apple ID.

Can I cancel the subscription anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, or via the App Store subscriptions page.
